The slots above the belt are the queue. Drag along the belt until the green cup is empty.

Tap a toy on a curved spur to send it. A spur that already holds a toy will not take another. Tap a toy at the elf to send it back.

Order does not matter. When the icons match, that elf clears and a new list appears. A full queue spends that elf's cheer. At 0, the next elf steps in.
